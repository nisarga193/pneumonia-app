Architecture

DenseNet-121 (ImageNet pretrained) with fine-tuned denseblock3 & 4. Custom 3-class head with dropout regularization.

Training used Weighted Random Sampler + class-weighted CrossEntropy (1.5× boost for pneumonia) to enforce high recall.

Grad-CAM hooks into features.denseblock4 to produce spatial activation maps showing which lung region the model focused on.

Dataset & Training

16,700+ X-rays across 3 classes: Normal, Bacterial Pneumonia, Viral Pneumonia.

Augmentations: random crop, horizontal flip, rotation ±10°, affine translate, brightness/contrast jitter.

Optimizer: AdamW with cosine annealing LR schedule. Separate learning rates — head: 1e-3, backbone: 1e-4. Early stopping based on macro recall, not just accuracy.

⚠️ Clinical Disclaimer: PneumoScan AI is an assistive tool only. It is not a substitute for professional medical diagnosis. All results must be reviewed and confirmed by a qualified physician before any clinical decisions are made. False negatives and false positives are possible.
