How It Works

1
Upload — Doctor uploads a chest X-ray (JPEG/PNG)
2
Preprocess — Resized to 224×224, normalized with ImageNet stats
3
Classify — DenseNet-121 outputs 3-class softmax probabilities
4
Grad-CAM — Gradients flow back to denseblock4 to generate heatmap
5
Display — X-ray + red heatmap overlay shown for doctor verification

Architecture

DenseNet-121 pretrained on ImageNet. Fine-tuned denseblock3 & 4. Custom 3-class head with dropout.

Weighted Random Sampler + 1.5× CrossEntropy boost for pneumonia classes.

Grad-CAM target layer: features.denseblock4

Dataset & Training

16,700+ X-rays: Normal, Bacterial Pneumonia, Viral Pneumonia.

Augmentations: random crop, flip, rotation ±10°, affine, contrast jitter.

AdamW + cosine LR annealing. Head LR: 1e-3, Backbone LR: 1e-4. Early stopping on macro recall.

⚠️ Clinical Disclaimer: PneumoScan AI is an assistive tool only. Not a substitute for professional medical diagnosis. All results must be reviewed by a qualified physician before clinical decisions are made.
